About

I'm a software engineer based in Toronto. With a co-founder, I built and shipped Boog, a native iOS app on the App Store, and ZeroPoint, a browser-based voice AI product that ran about 100 testing sessions before pivoting. On my own, I built Clovor, a full-stack AI platform on AWS.

Across these projects, the recurring work has been the parts that have to stay correct in production: concurrency-safe playback and storage, deterministic state around unpredictable LLM output, tenant isolation enforced at the data layer, and CI/CD that authenticates without long-lived credentials.

I'm looking for software engineering roles across full-stack, backend, frontend, cloud, AI application and iOS work.
